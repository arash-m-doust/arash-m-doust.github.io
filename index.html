Research Focus

AI-assisted concept-to-execution for design and the built environment

A persistent gap separates expressive design concepts from buildable, documented, fabrication-oriented output. My current research direction examines how generative and multimodal AI can help close that gap responsibly, with the designer retaining control of the outcome.

Concept-to-fabrication & concept-to-BIM workflows. Translating concept imagery into dimensioned, materially-specified, documentation-oriented output.
Composable generative-AI pipelines for design. Architectures and methods for combining models into reliable, end-to-end design tooling.
Verification & fabrication-readiness. Evaluation methods for the quality and buildability of AI-generated design documentation.
Designer agency in AI-augmented practice. How tools should distribute control between human and system in professional design work.

Flagship Research Artifact

Detail Design Agent / AIQ Studio

An early research prototype investigating an AI pipeline that transforms a 2D concept image, moodboard, or reference into engineering-oriented output: dimensioned blueprints, orthographic, sectional and exploded views, material recommendations, fabrication logic, and render-ready 3D meshes.

The problem · The approach

From designer vision to execution reality

Concept visuals are rich but lack dimensions, material specifications, assembly and fabrication logic, and manufacturability data — leaving a gap between design and the factory/BIM floor. The prototype explores vision-language models, monocular depth estimation, 3D reconstruction, and generative technical drawing to narrow it. It is an early proof of concept and a foundation for future evaluation in AI-assisted concept-to-execution.

Repository documentation is being prepared.
