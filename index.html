01 Research Interest

My work sits between architectural practice and computational technology. Building on a background in architecture, building energy, and BIM, I develop and study methods for generating and — above all — verifying building design documentation produced by computational and AI-assisted tools. I am seeking a funded PhD position in construction informatics and building-performance computation, addressing a practical question: how can digitally generated technical documents be made trustworthy, engineering-valid, and performance-aware for use in real practice.
